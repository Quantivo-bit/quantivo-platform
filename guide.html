THE V4 WORKSPACE GUIDE

A clear starting point.

Get comfortable with the workflow. Know what each tool does.

Your first five minutes

  1. Choose Register to create an account, or Explore without an account for the guest workspace. If accounts are unavailable on a preview, the form says so.
  2. Open Market watch, search for an asset and use the star to save a favorite.
  3. Try a suggested question in Research copilot. Responses are scripted, not AI-generated market analysis.
  4. Run a moving-average crossover in Backtest lab. Change the windows and fees, then compare results.
  5. Review and confirm a virtual order in Paper portfolio. Export its record from Trade journal.

On a phone, use the menu button at the top. Signing in after choosing a feature takes you directly to that feature.

Accounts and guest mode

When the account service is connected, registration creates a server account with a separate paper journal, favorites and price rules. Use the same email and password to return on another device. Sessions last up to seven days. Sign out ends the current session.

Use a password or passphrase of 15–128 characters. Email verification, password recovery, MFA, subscriptions and payments are not available in this beta.

Guest activity stays in this browser and is not transferred to a registered account. Clearing browser storage removes it.

Importing a dataset

CSV files need timestamp,open,high,low,close; volume is optional. Use increasing ISO dates or Unix timestamps and valid positive OHLC values. Files are limited to 2 MB and 30–10,000 candles. Download the sample in the lab for the exact format.

CSV data stays in browser memory and is used only for backtesting. Paper trading uses fixed sample quotes.

What a backtest calculates

A long-only simple-moving-average crossover. A fast average crossing above the slow average schedules a buy at the next candle's open. The opposite crossing schedules a sell. Fees apply on both sides. Any final open position is liquidated at the last close.

Results include net return, closed trades, maximum drawdown and a buy-and-hold comparison. Spread, slippage, funding, liquidity limits and outages are not modeled. Sample candles are generated, not historical market data.

Privacy and usage notes

Registered accounts store a display name, email address, salted password hash and account activity on the site's server. A session cookie keeps you signed in. Do not enter broker passwords, wallet secrets or API keys in this workspace.

Guest orders, favorites and rules are stored in this browser. Research prompts, alert-check history, CSV data and backtest results stay in memory. There are no advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting providers may keep access logs. GitHub links open a separate service.

Reset demo removes paper trades, rules and favorites from the current workspace only. Export the journal first if you want a copy. Reset does not delete a registered account. Account deletion and recovery interfaces are not yet available.

Research comes with uncertainty

Prices and assistant responses are illustrative. This workspace provides research and simulation tools, sends no live orders and does not offer personalized investment advice. Simulated results do not establish future profitability.
