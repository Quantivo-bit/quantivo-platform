07 / FAQ

Clear answers before the first signal.

Does Quantivo send real orders?

The public project is paper-trading focused. A future broker connection will be introduced only after controlled testing and explicit approval.

Is Quantivo an investment adviser?

No. Quantivo is a technical research and simulation project. Its outputs are not personalized investment advice or a guarantee of performance.

Which assets are supported?

The current research universe is configured around BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT, with SOL/USDT, BNB/USDT and XRP/USDT planned as independent model tracks.

Can I add another symbol?

Yes. The application is designed so a new symbol can be added through configuration, followed by separate data preparation, model validation and paper-trading review.
