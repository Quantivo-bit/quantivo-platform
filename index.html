Read the quick guide ↗
What can I actually do in this demo?

Explore sample markets, use a scripted research assistant, backtest a moving-average strategy on sample or uploaded CSV data, place virtual orders, manage price rules and export your paper-trade journal.

Is the research copilot connected to AI?

Not yet. It provides scripted, context-aware demonstrations using sample data. A real AI connection and your Python/XGBoost model integration are planned and are not connected in this release.

Are the prices and performance figures live?

No. The market views use clearly marked synthetic sample data. The backtest lab can also calculate results from your own uploaded CSV. Neither sample results nor historical tests predict future returns.

Does Quantivo place real trades?

This version never sends orders to a broker or exchange. Paper orders only change your virtual workspace balance. cTrader integration is planned.

Where are my data and demo settings stored?

Signed-in users save paper trades, watchlists and rules to their account. Guest activity stays in this browser. Imported CSV files and backtest calculations stay on your device.

Can I receive alerts when I close the site?

Not in this release. Rules are checked when you click the sample-check button. Background scheduling, email and Telegram delivery are planned backend capabilities.
